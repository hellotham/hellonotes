# Organising

Three ways, and you can use all of them at once.

## Folders

Real directories. The sidebar mirrors them, and dragging a note moves the file.
There is an [[Examples/Nested Note|example in a subfolder]].

## Tags

Write `#like-this` anywhere in the body, or list them in front matter as this
note does. The **Tags** place collects every tag in the collection. This note is
tagged `#tour` and `#demo`.

## Properties

The YAML block at the top of this file is **front matter**, and the Properties
inspector edits it as typed fields rather than raw text. Open it and you will see
`title`, `tags` and `priority` as editable rows.

Anything HelloNotes writes for you — a summary, suggested tags, suggested links —
goes into front matter, never into your prose. **The app does not rewrite your
body text.**
