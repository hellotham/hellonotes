# The right panel

The window is three regions, each about one thing: **the collection on the
left, the note in the middle, and what the note is on the right.**

One button at the top right shows or hides the panel. Along the top of the
panel is a row of icons — everything it can show about the open note — and the
one you are looking at is highlighted. One thing at a time, and the note beside
it stays live: you can keep typing while the panel is open.

| Panel | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary & Outline** | the AI summary, headings, word count and reading time |
| **Tags** | this note's tags, and the collection's |
| **Links** | backlinks, outgoing links, unlinked mentions |
| **Properties** | front matter as typed, editable fields |
| **History** | Git versions of this note, if it is in a repository |
| **Graph** | this note's links in and out — and a link or two further, if you like |

A command that produces an answer — Summarise, Suggest Tags, Suggest Links —
opens the panel at the place its answer lands.

## About the collection

What is about the whole collection is in the sidebar, at the top: the
**Mind Map** of the links across it, the **Assistant**, **Ask Your Library**,
and its **Git**. The first three open as tabs beside your notes, so a note and
the Mind Map, or a note and the Assistant, are a tab apart.
