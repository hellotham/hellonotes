# Settings

On a Mac, **HelloNotes ▸ Settings…** (**⌘,**). On iPad, the **⋯** button in the
toolbar, or **⌘,** with a keyboard; on iPhone, the **⋯** button in the Notes tab.

## Appearance

Light, dark, or follow the system, and increased contrast. An accent colour and
the text size. Reading and editor widths, a wrap guide, how notes are sorted, and
whether a note shows its title.

## Folders

Where pasted images go — a subfolder, or beside the note — how daily notes are
named and which folder they go in, and the templates folder. On a Mac these are
under **General**.

## AI

Choose the model for the Assistant and the model for the writing tools — System
or an MLX model — each with a line saying where your text goes. Choose the models
folder, use or remove the MLX models in it, or download one by its Hugging Face
name. Set the Assistant's creativity. Turn suggestions as you type on or off.

Everything that says **AI Settings…** — in the Assistant, or the AI place on
iPhone — opens this page.

## Git

Your commit identity, and accounts for hosts you push to. On iPhone and iPad this
appears while a collection is open.

## Support

HelloNotes is free and every feature is included for everyone; it is paid for
by the people who choose to. Backing it also lets you send a support request
from inside the app. See [[Manual/Supporting HelloNotes]].
