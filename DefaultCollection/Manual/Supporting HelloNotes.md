# Supporting HelloNotes

HelloNotes is free, and **every feature is included for everyone**. Backing the
app adds one thing, and it is not a feature: you can send a support request from
inside the app.

## Champion

A one-off contribution from people who want the app to keep being made. You can
give more than once — the app remembers how many times, because someone who has
backed it five times has done something worth acknowledging.

## Commercial

An annual licence for using HelloNotes at work. Same app, same features — it is
a licence, not an unlock, in the tradition of tools that stay free for
individuals and ask companies to pay.

## Support requests

Champion supporters and commercial licence holders can open a support request in
**Settings ▸ Support**. It is a way to reach the person who makes HelloNotes —
not a faster queue, and nothing in the app behaves differently either way.

The app sends nothing itself. It writes the message, adds the app and system
versions, and hands the whole thing to your own mail app, where you can read
every word — and delete any of it — before you press send. Nothing about your
notes is included: no folder names, no file names, no text.

Everything above lives in **Settings ▸ Support**.
