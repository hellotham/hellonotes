# Start Here

Welcome. **This collection is a real folder of Markdown files on your device** —
open it in any other editor and you will find exactly what you see here. That is
the whole idea: HelloNotes is a good way to read and write plain files you
already own, not a place your writing goes to live.

Everything below is a note you can edit, break, and delete. Nothing here is
special.

## If you only do one thing

Press **⌘N** and start typing. Then press **⌘O** and choose a folder of your
own — this collection has done its job the moment you have your own notes open.

> [!note] You can close this collection
> It is not special. Close it from the sidebar whenever you like, and reopen it
> later with **File ▸ Open Default Collection**.
