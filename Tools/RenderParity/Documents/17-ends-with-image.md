# The gate latch

Third attempt. The first two failed because the post has moved about four
millimetres since the gate was hung, and I kept trying to adjust the latch
rather than the keeper.

Moving the keeper took ten minutes. Adjusting the latch took two weekends.

The lesson, which I will forget: measure the thing that moved, not the thing
that stopped working.
