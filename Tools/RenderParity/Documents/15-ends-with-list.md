# Packing — Tasmania, five days

Cold mornings, warm afternoons, rain at any point. Everything has to fit in the
carry-on, so the boots get worn rather than packed.

Laundry once, mid-trip, which is what keeps the shirt count down.

- Boots (worn)
- Two shirts
- One warm layer
  - the merino one, not the fleece
  - the fleece takes twice the space and dries slower
- Rain shell
- Three days of socks
- Charger, one cable, the small adapter
